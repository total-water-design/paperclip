True Batch ROInitially charged inventory concentrates as permeate leaves.No continuous fresh-feed mixing into the concentrating inventory during productive concentration.Transient inventory with converged quasi-steady increments; not valve dynamics, CFD or fatigue modelling.

Eligible brackish-water energy recovery

Total RO Design evaluates brackish-water ERD configurations where useful concentrate pressure remains available, including multistage isobaric exchange, turbo, Pelton and interstage alternatives subject to topology and release eligibility.

Entitlement is not engineering eligibility. Feature access does not declare an ERD feasible: pressure, flow, device range, topology and operating constraints must also be satisfied. Pump and ERD vendor curves require confirmation.

THE PLATFORM

Specialist Engineering Without Fragmented Project Context.

The Suite combines specialist applications that own process calculations with shared services and contracts that preserve project meaning between them.

The Shared Project Foundation

Account, access and shell

Controlled access, common navigation, appearance, project controls, feedback, security and status language reduce relearning.

Project identity and revision

Projects can retain a common prefix, project number, design case and revision history.

WaterStream and chemistry

Controlled stream and chemistry foundations preserve composition, conditions, provenance, diagnostics, alkalinity and saturation context where applicable.

Economics and snapshots

Specialists can contribute process-scope costs and provenance; reports are tied to solved, versioned results.

Each Application Owns Its Physics

ApplicationOwns
PretreatmentUnit operations, cleaning systems, hydraulics and residuals transformations
BioBiological kinetics, mass balance, aeration, sludge and equipment logic
ROMembrane transport, staging, hydraulics, pressure, pumps, ERDs and energy
ZLDConcentration, thermal duty, evaporation, crystallization and solids pathways
Water BalanceTopology, closure, recycle accounting and diagnostics
EconomicsCost ownership, lifecycle economics and finance calculations
System IntegrationOrchestration, comparison and optimization over validated results

Preserve Meaning at Every Handoff

A controlled handoff may include project/revision, application/version, full-precision stream and chemistry state, solids/phase, assumptions, warnings, maturity, economic summary and provenance. It should not silently refresh, reduce required chemistry to TDS alone or use rounded display values as a calculation state.

Progressive Integration, Clearly Disclosed

The common platform and engineering contracts are being implemented progressively. The deeper engineering contracts—WaterStream, shared chemistry, economic summaries and cross-application lineage—are being adopted progressively. Handoffs are identified as available, in engineering preview, awaiting validation or planned.

The Complete Engineering Workflow Being Built

Source Water → Pretreatment alternatives → Membrane or biological treatment → Reuse, concentrate or ZLD pathways → Plant-wide balance closure → Lifecycle economics → System comparison and optimization → Versioned engineering report.

The engineer chooses cases, constraints, accepted assumptions, maturity and final recommendation.
