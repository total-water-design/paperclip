RO MODE AND ERD MATURITY

One membrane basis. Multiple process architectures.

Current: conventional one- to four-stage RO in Total RO Design v0.25. Engineering preview / under validation: True Batch RO, Semi-Batch RO, isobaric exchange, turbo, Pelton and interstage ERD paths. Planned: advanced optimization. Availability does not make every mode or ERD path current.

ModeInventory behaviorFresh make-up behaviorEngineering boundary
Conventional RO — currentContinuous feed-to-concentrate flow through staged membrane arrays.Continuous feed forms the operating stream.Steady operating condition with stated hydraulic, membrane and chemistry assumptions.
True Batch RO — previewInitially charged inventory concentrates as permeate leaves.No continuous fresh-feed mixing into the concentrating inventory during productive concentration.Transient inventory with converged quasi-steady increments; not valve dynamics, CFD or fatigue modelling.
Semi-Batch RO — under validationConcentrate recirculates and loop inventory stays approximately constant during recirculation.Fresh make-up enters while permeate is produced; displacement, recirculation and purge are a complete sequence.Full chemistry is needed to review high-recovery scaling feasibility.

Eligible brackish-water energy recovery

Preview paths evaluate brackish-water ERD configurations only where useful concentrate pressure remains available, including multistage isobaric exchange, turbo, Pelton and interstage alternatives. Pump and ERD vendor curves require confirmation.

Entitlement is not engineering eligibility. Feature access does not declare an ERD feasible: pressure, flow, device range, topology and operating constraints must also be satisfied.

Pretreatment basis and boundaries

Current engineering-preview UF/MF cleaning basis: 3–9 h focused CEB/CIP study. The 6–20 h conceptual train is planned-engine scope. Planned train elements include coagulation, sludge thickening and filter press.

Current OEM/vendor instructions supersede stored references. No unpublished cleaner is auto-populated; specialty CEB requires product/OEM approval. Project-specific losses control hydraulics, and unsupported removal remains blank or an explicit user assumption.

{% endif %} {% if product.product_id == 'bio' %}

Bio process-family breadth and MBR maturity

Conceptual activated sludge, MBR, aeration, sludge and reuse framing are in development; detailed MBR planning is engineering preview, not a released performance guarantee.

MBR preview exposes editable defaults for gross-to-net flux, cycles/downtime, membrane area, N+1 trains, modules/racks, offline flux, air, pumps and cleaning. Inputs/outputs preserve BOD/COD/TSS, nutrients, treated-effluent/sludge context and handoff limits: BOD/COD/TSS cannot infer ions for downstream RO. Activity ranges: 6–18 h conceptual study; 10–28 h detailed MBR planning.

{% endif %} {% if product.product_id == 'zld' %}

ZLD screening boundaries

FFE engineering preview applies only to 280–305 K, 35,000–95,000 mg/L, Reynolds number 45–90 and Prandtl number 5–10. It is a horizontal-tube correlation, not a universal VFFE model.

Crystallization is screening only: multi-salt behavior, sequence, recycle and kinetics require validation. Non-condensables, vacuum, entrainment, vapor body, shell/tubesheet, metallurgy, fabrication, pilot work and vendor mechanical design are excluded.

{% endif %} {% if product.product_id == 'balance' %}

Water Balance declaration and handoff states

Owners declare generation, consumption, dose and accumulation. Serialization and model hashing are immutable and deterministic; diagnostics identify the largest residual. The balance does not average pH, infer partitioning, invent mass or hide a non-converged recycle. The pure-aqueous recycle qualifier remains explicit.

Handoffs are labelled available, engineering preview, awaiting validation or planned.

{% endif %} {% if product.product_id == 'economics' %}

Economic basis and financial boundary

Use native/reporting currency with FX rate, source and snapshot; record estimate date, maturity and case lineage. No silent 1:1 conversion is used. Total Project Cost, Total Capital Requirement, economic LCOW, finance/target-DSCR tariff, customer price, project/equity IRR, and DSCR/LLCR/PLCR are separate measures.

Specialists own process-scope costs; the project owns project-wide costs. Lifecycle screening is separate from advanced development finance. Shared FX, linkage, tax adapters, sculpting, cash sweep and hedging remain planned.

Specialist Engineering Without Fragmented Project Context.
