WHY THE SUITE EXISTS

A Treatment Plant Is a System. Its Design Software Should Work the Same Way.

Engineers repeatedly move between source-water characterization, pretreatment, membranes, biological treatment, balances, concentrate management, energy and lifecycle economics. The Suite is being built to reduce repeated entry, unit-conversion errors, inconsistent assumptions and report reconstruction without collapsing every technology into one generic model.

WHAT INTEGRATED MEANS

Integrated Does Not Mean One Oversimplified Solver.

One project identity

Applications can share a project family, design case and revision.

Shared stream and chemistry basis

Specialist ownership

Traceable handoffs

The common platform and engineering contracts are being implemented progressively. Integration coverage varies by application and release. Every product page identifies what is available now, what is under validation and what remains planned.
