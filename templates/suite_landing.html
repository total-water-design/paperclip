A MESSAGE FROM THE FOUNDER

Engineering should be spent solving water challenges - not reconciling disconnected tools.

Over more than 20 years working in international sales and business development for hydraulic equipment and water infrastructure, I repeatedly saw engineers move between membrane programs, pump tools, energy-recovery calculations, spreadsheets, chemistry packages and economic models.

Each tool may be capable on its own. The inefficiency comes from the gaps between them. When one assumption changes, engineers often have to update several applications, reconcile different units and design bases, and repeat work that should remain connected.

That experience led to the vision for the Total Water Design Suite: specialized engineering applications that preserve their technical depth while sharing one account, one project identity and, progressively, validated streams and assumptions across the complete treatment train.

The objective is not to replace engineering judgment. It is to create a better environment in which qualified professionals can apply it - from pretreatment and biological treatment through membranes, demineralization, brine concentration, water balance, economics and system-level optimization.

My tenure as President of ALADyR further reinforced the scale of that need. The water industry is being asked to deliver more capacity, greater efficiency and increasingly sophisticated reuse and desalination systems while controlling capital and operating cost.

We are building the Suite to help engineers move from a design question to an informed, traceable decision with less unnecessary iteration.

Jerry Ross Founder & Chief Executive Officer Total Water Design Suite Former President, ALADyR

ENGINEERING BASIS

Transparent preliminary-engineering software

Engineering responsibility

The applications provide preliminary engineering estimates and decision support. Qualified professionals must independently verify inputs, assumptions, equipment limits and results before design, procurement, operation, contractual reliance or investment.

Product status

The Suite is being built progressively. Only products marked Available provide active access. Coming Soon and In Development cards are roadmap information, not release promises.

Vendor-neutral intent

Where the underlying data and calculation engine support it, the Suite is designed to enable transparent, vendor-neutral comparison without representing itself as a manufacturer projection or guarantee.
