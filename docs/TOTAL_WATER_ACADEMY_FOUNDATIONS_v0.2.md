# Total Water Academy — Engineering Foundations & Placement v0.2

**Owner:** `app/total-water-academy`  
**Course version:** 0.2.0  
**Guided engagement:** 360 hours across ten levels

## Why Unit Operations is foundational

Total Water Academy teaches water-treatment design as applied process engineering. Students should understand the physical and chemical principles behind equipment before they learn how to operate specialist Suite applications.

Level 2 is therefore a dedicated **42-hour Core Unit Operations, Balances & Transport Fundamentals** block before Pretreatment, Membranes, Biological Treatment and ZLD.

It covers:

1. engineering units, dimensional analysis and process variables;
2. total mass, component and water balances;
3. energy balances and heat transfer;
4. fluid flow, momentum, pressure and headloss;
5. mass transfer, diffusion and separation fundamentals;
6. chemical equilibrium, reaction and water-chemistry foundations.

The intent is not to reproduce a full chemical-engineering degree. It is to give every learner enough process-engineering foundation to understand why a DAF, membrane, aeration basin, heat exchanger, evaporator, pump or crystallizer behaves as it does.

## Connection to the Suite

Fundamental teaching should progressively connect to validated Suite calculations. Mass/water-balance exercises should use Total Water Balance / canonical WaterStream where appropriate. Aqueous-chemistry exercises should use Shared Water Chemistry where appropriate. Specialist design calculations remain owned by Pretreatment, RO, Bio, ZLD and other specialist branches.

Where the Suite does not yet expose an authoritative calculation adapter, Academy may teach standard engineering principles and worked examples, clearly labeled as educational assumptions. It must not create a competing professional calculation engine.

## Entry knowledge diagnostic

A learner entering Academy for the first time is offered a short diagnostic consisting of:

- background and years of relevant experience;
- learning objective;
- self-rating in major engineering domains;
- a 12-question concept check.

The diagnostic samples:

- engineering units;
- mass/component balance;
- energy balance and heat transfer;
- fluid flow/hydraulics;
- mass transfer;
- chemistry;
- treatment-process reasoning;
- electrical and process-control fundamentals.

The result assigns one of three guidance tracks:

- **Foundation Builder** — more explanation, worked examples and guided practice;
- **Engineering Refresher** — normal course flow with targeted review;
- **Accelerated Review** — concise foundation review before deeper design missions.

These tracks change instructional emphasis, not completion requirements.

## No automatic credit by self-report

Professional experience or self-confidence alone must never waive a module. The diagnostic is advisory. All learners pursuing the Academy diploma remain subject to the same required module and capstone completion rules unless a future formally validated challenge-assessment policy is approved.

The diagnostic may be skipped temporarily and retaken later.

## Future learning adaptation

As the Academy matures, the stored knowledge profile can be used to:

- show optional foundation refreshers;
- recommend extra practice in weak domains;
- shorten explanations for advanced learners without hiding content;
- recommend prerequisite modules before specialist exercises;
- compare entry and exit competency growth;
- support future validated challenge assessments if explicitly approved.

It must not be used to infer professional licensure, employment fitness, or academic accreditation.
